🍉 Melon Squish

Semi-fluid watermelons squish, flow, merge and split. Keep the jar from overflowing!

  • Aim & tap to drop the next melon.
  • Same-size melons touching ooze together into a bigger one.
  • Press & drag inside the jar to squish melons into gaps.
  • Swipe fast through a melon to slice it in half (costs a knife; merges sharpen new ones).
  • Stay under the red line. Merge two golden melons for a JUICE BOMB!
